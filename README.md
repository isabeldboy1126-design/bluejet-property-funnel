# Real Estate Lead-Conversion Funnel (Demo)

A high-converting, mobile-first lead capture and buyer qualification funnel designed for a Nigerian real estate firm. Built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and React Router, closely styled after the clean, centered, premium UI reference.

---

## 1. Landing Page Structure (Exact Section Order)

1. **Hero Section (Optimized Above-the-Fold)**
   - Headline size reduced to ~54px desktop / ~34px mobile with tight leading (~0.98):
     *“Achieve [DESIRED PROPERTY OUTCOME] With More Confidence”*
   - Selective primary green (`#133E2B`) outcome emphasis.
   - Clean supporting copy & value points.
   - Primary CTA: *“Get the Free Guide”* & microcopy.
   - **Compact, lightweight proof row fully visible above the fold**:
     *“XX+ YEARS”* | *“XXX+”* | *“PROOF SIGNAL”*.
2. **Company / Authority Section**
   - Editorial 2-column layout (no card grid).
   - Left: *“Who’s Behind This Guide”* & *“Created by [COMPANY NAME]”*.
   - Right: 3 compact typographic metric dividers.
3. **Problem Section (Side-by-Side on Desktop)**
   - Editorial treatment with green emphasis:
     *“Avoid the Problems That Make Property Decisions Harder”*
   - 3 columns side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile.
   - Natural copy (Risk / How This Guide Helps) + individual contextual CTAs.
4. **Benefits Section (Transformation Outcomes)**
   - **Full-width Deep Forest Green (`#133E2B`)** section with light typography.
   - Asymmetric editorial layout with vertical numbered transformation list (`01`–`04`) and dividers.
   - Contextual CTA: *“Get the Resource to Achieve [DESIRED PROPERTY OUTCOME]”*.
5. **What’s Inside the Guide (Features & Tools)**
   - Editorial numbered rows with green emphasis:
     *“What You’ll Learn Before Taking Your Next Step”*
   - Natural copy topics `01` through `04` + contextual CTA.
6. **Optional Company Social Proof**
   - Focuses strictly on results from the company behind the guide.
   - Asymmetric layout: 1 large featured quote + 2 supporting quotes.
   - Fully toggleable in configuration.
7. **Final CTA**
   - Deep green container with headline green/light emphasis:
     *“Get the Information You Need to Move Forward With Confidence”*
   - Clean body and *“Get the Free Guide”* button.

## 3. Buyer Qualification Success Page (Benefit-Oriented Website Bridge)

Hierarchy:
1. **Details Received (Simple Confirmation)**:
   - *“Thanks, [FIRST NAME]”*
   - *“We’ve received your details.”*
2. **Normal Clean 2-Column Grid**:
   - **Card 1 (Website Bridge)**:
     - Eyebrow: `WHILE YOU WAIT`
     - Heading: `Benefit-oriented reason to continue to the website`
     - Body: `What the prospect can see, understand or compare on the website that helps them make a better property decision.`
     - Primary Action: `Outcome-oriented CTA` &rarr; links to website.
   - **Card 2 (Direct Advisor Contact)**:
     - Eyebrow: `DIRECT CONTACT`
     - Heading: `Talk to an Advisor`
     - Body: `Prefer direct assistance right away? Connect with our advisory team to discuss specific requirements.`
     - Action: `Talk to an Advisor`
